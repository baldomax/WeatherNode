# About WeatherNode

Last updated: 2026-02-10

WeatherNode is an open-source weather platform focused on local weather stations, clear dashboards, and community-friendly data sharing.

![WeatherNode logo](/images/logo_full_256w.png)

## What WeatherNode Does

- collects and displays local station data
- combines local data with forecast and environmental sources
- provides configurable widgets, maps, alerts, and history
- supports optional community station telemetry

## Project Principles

- transparency in data handling
- open-source collaboration
- practical reliability for day-to-day weather insight
- clear separation between official project channels and community forks

## Weather Information Disclaimer

Weather data and forecasts can be delayed, incomplete, or inaccurate.

- Use this site for informational purposes only.
- Do not rely on it as the sole basis for safety-critical decisions (e.g. severe weather response, aviation, marine, property protection).
- For official warnings and emergency guidance, follow your local meteorological agency and authorities.

## Open Source and Community

WeatherNode is developed in the open.  
Source code, issue tracking, and releases are managed through the official project repositories and website channels.
